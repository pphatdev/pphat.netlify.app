# ✨ **About Project**

red-ant-express.com.kh is a business-focused logistics website for Red Ant Express Co., Ltd.
The platform presents service categories, delivery value propositions, company contact channels, and customer support links in a clear and responsive layout.
It is designed to help visitors quickly understand delivery options such as food, express, and standard shipping, while guiding them to tracking and contact actions.

# 🛠️ **Project Tech Stack**

- **Core:**
  - JavaScript
  - HTML5
  - CSS3

- **UI & Styling:**
  - Responsive web layout
  - Service cards and section-based content blocks
  - Corporate visual identity and branded components

- **Features & Utilities:**
  - Service category presentation
  - Delivery workflow highlights
  - Contact and support information sections
  - Social links and policy pages

# 🌈 **Project Features**

- Corporate logistics landing page
- Clear service segmentation (Food, Express, Standard)
- Mobile app and technology promotion section
- Tracking and delivery-oriented call-to-action flow
- Contact details and location visibility
- Responsive browsing experience across devices
